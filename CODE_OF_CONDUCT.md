# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Lumina project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this community:

- Being kind and patient with people who are new to image formats or to
  JavaScript.
- Giving technical criticism about the code, never about the person.
- Accepting that maintainers may decline a change, and explaining the
  disagreement with evidence (a failing test, a reference file, a spec quote).
- Crediting other people's work — including tools like psd-tools and Pillow that
  this project depends on for verification.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Public or private harassment, or publishing others' private information
  without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages) and when an individual is representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour by opening a
private report through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
flow, or by contacting the maintainer listed in [package.json](package.json).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining consequences, from a
private warning, through temporary restriction from interaction, to a permanent
ban from the project.
